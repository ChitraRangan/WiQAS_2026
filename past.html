Event details

The 2025 symposium

Date: Friday, July 11, 2025
Time: 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.
Venue: Ed Lumley CEI, Room 1101, University of Windsor

Looking back

A year of quantum science.

The event marked the 2025 International Year of Quantum Science and Technology and brought together University of Windsor faculty with external speakers from academia, industry, technology organizations, and innovation partners.
