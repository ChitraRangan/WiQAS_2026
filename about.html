About WiQAS

Quantum applications.
Across disciplines.

The Windsor Quantum Applications Symposium brings researchers, students, engineers, scientists, and external partners together to discuss quantum technologies and their applications.

An interdisciplinary forum

Space to exchange ideas.

Expert talks, poster presentations, networking, and discussions about collaboration.

Q

Quantum computing

Algorithms, programming environments, architectures, and emerging computational workflows.

S

Quantum sensing

Quantum-enabled measurement concepts and sensing platforms.

A

Applications

Cross-disciplinary uses connecting quantum methods with engineering, scientific data, and security.

Who should attend?

Faculty, researchers, graduate and undergraduate students, industry professionals, technology organizations, innovation partners, and anyone interested in quantum technologies and their applications.
